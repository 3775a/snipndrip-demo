Your data & POPIA
We collect your ID document, a selfie, and location only to verify identity and coordinate a booking safely. It's encrypted at rest, never sold, and only shared with your matched stylist or customer once a booking is confirmed. In line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you can request a copy of what we hold or ask us to delete it from your Profile at any time.
